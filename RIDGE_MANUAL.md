# FIRE-TOWER 2 — WATCH CREW FIELD MANUAL (Dead Ridge)

> Recovered from the desk drawer of Fire-Tower 2. The previous watchman underlined
> half of it. Where the underlining stops, so does the paper.

---

## 1. YOUR SHIFT

You are the 30-day fire watch for the Dead Ridge sector. Mark (systems),
Chloe (survival) and Sam (photography) are your crew. The previous ranger,
**Arthur Pendelton**, walked off six days before you arrived. His final log reads:
*"The barometers are lying. They are mimicking the pressure."*

Each in-game day gives you **two actions** before night falls (REST advances
time). You **must** submit an enviro-log every day. A shift is judged at 06:00
on Day 30.

## 2. THE LIVING WINDOW (center viewport)

The center screen is not decoration — it is your primary instrument. It renders
**wherever you currently are**: the tower cabin, the Copperhead Bridge, the
Mining Shaft, the ruins, the sinkhole, the impossible clearing. Travel and the
viewport travels with you.

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Trees swaying, wind metric low | Something is moving them |
| Fresh scratches, three-beat rhythm | It has been on the catwalk — going **up**, not down |
| Tower 3 blinking green | Morse. H-E-L-P. Do not investigate at night |
| Fog thickening fast | Awareness is high; it is closing distance |
| A companion outside who is inside | **The glass is lying.** Sanity is below 50 |
| Windows lit on dead towers | Perception fracture — verify with binoculars |
| The window fully black for seconds | Lantern failure. Or not |

**Focus Binoculars** (Watch menu, once per day) gives a *verified* observation
while Sanity ≥ 50. Below that, the binoculars start lying too — and sometimes
something notices you looking. The 7-segment shows **U** while focused.

## 3. BAROMETRIC CALIBRATION & LOG ENGINE (ForestOS laptop)

Open the laptop (LAPTOP in the tower hub). Amber overlay = ForestOS v4.2.

1. **CALIBRATION** — steer the digital deviation to ±2 mb using `<< < > >>`,
   then LOCK SYNC (SPACE).
   - NORTH + VALLEY arrays are always honest.
   - **SOUTH = 1013 mb / MIMICRY INTERFERENCE** means the creature is exhaling
     onto the sensor. Exclude it: the honest median is then (North+Valley)/2.
   - Locking onto the fake value pops the lower vent hatches. Don't.
2. **LOG ENGINE** — enter the exact validation string (`DR-##-###`) once synced.
   Missed days cost endings. `ARTHUR SENT ME` is a joke. A joke the terminal
   finds funny.
3. **INBOX** — forestry emails arrive on schedule. They are mostly lies that
   tell the truth sideways.
4. **WEATHERRADAR** — a 300 m void signature sits over the tower and moves
   *against* the wind from Day 8. It is not weather.

The weather station (`W` key / combo **2+4**) shows the same sensor grid
standalone, with live pressure readouts and anomaly tracking.

## 4. THE SHORTWAVE — LIFELINE AND BAIT

Channels 14 / 88 / 104. **Every listen feeds the learning meter.** When it
crosses ~55, the entity hijacks channels and speaks in your companions' own
voices, telling you things only they know.

> **RULE OF THREE: if it speaks your name, don't answer.**
> Answering validates its placement in the environment (+22 learning, -20 sanity).
> Kill the breaker. Silence is protection.

## 5. CREW & CAST — duties and warning signs

| Who | Duty | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|
| **Mark** | Terminal, calibration (+4s buffer on his shift) | Asthma whistles below 960mb; loses it under pressure |
| **Chloe** | Water, perimeter, forage (+25% yields) | Counts eyes at the fence; will attack the deadbolts if her family's voice appears |
| **Sam** | Camera, flash defense, night scouting | Wanders off-frame; +15 Awareness whenever he "sees something" |
| **Dr. Elena Voss** | Acoustics research; the decoy tape plan | Finishing your sentences — correctly |
| **Ash** | Morale anchor (+15 sanity), knows the clearing | Humming songs nobody taught them |
| **Ranger Cole** | Dispatch relief, Day 13+ | Recites your logs back. Ask him things only Cole knows. Watch his boots |

Use **Crew Check-in** (Watch menu): companions speak from their live state —
sanity band, who is alive, what just happened. They remember. They repeat
themselves less. They break in character.

## 6. THE OVERWORLD (map: Tab / combo 1+2)

Daylight is survivable; **night outside is a decision, not a default.** The
world evolves as you play — bridge cables snap, Tower 1 goes dark, the cabins
burn without fire, a clearing appears south of the sinkhole, Mining Shaft 4's
gate stands open and warm. Locations render in the viewport with their own
composition, fog and silhouettes.

New encounters appear with the campaign: Voss in the root cellar (Day ≥ 8),
Ash in the clearing (after the world shift), Cole on the ladder (Day ≥ 13).

## 7. AUDIO — WHAT THE CABIN SOUNDS LIKE

- Low forest bed, thicker with fog and night.
- Heartbeat pulse when Sanity < 45.
- Glass scratching when Awareness ≥ 35.
- Almost-words in the wind — band-shaped syllables that never resolve.
- **Dead air**: rare total silence below 35 Sanity. Worse than noise.
- Jumpscare: the screen dies black for 300 ms, then it is at the glass.
- The buzzer activates itself in three beats below 40 Sanity.
- The 7-segment displays wrong characters (`8 _ -`) at low Sanity. It's not the
  hardware.

## 8. CONTROLS & COMBOS

`1-4` choices · `SPACE` confirm · `Tab` map · `W` weather station · `G` guide
· Combos: `1+2` map · `3+4` guide · `1+4` more stories · `2+4` weather ·
`2+3` mute. Physical deck mirrors everything; combos work on the Pi buttons too
(red+yellow = story page).
