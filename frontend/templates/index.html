📟 CHANNEL 7 — OPERATOR'S FIELD MANUAL

① THE LOOP

You wake behind a barricade (or a fire-tower hatch) every dawn. Each day you get 2 actions — scavenge, craft, fortify, talk — then night falls. Survive, manage hunger/thirst/infection, and push toward your run's finish line (Day 21 extraction in the classic story; Day 30 in Dead Ridge).

② THE BUTTONS

1 / 2 / 3 / 4 (red/blue/green/yellow) pick the matching on-screen choice. SPACE hits the labelled utility button (REST, BACK, LOCK SYNC, MEDKIT…). The keyboard and the physical arcade deck send identical commands.

③ BUTTON COMBINATIONS

Press two arcade keys together (or their keyboard twins):
1+2 → toggle the reconnaissance MAP · 3+4 → this GUIDE · 1+4 → next STORY page on run start · 2+3 → mute/unmute audio · 2+4 → DEAD RIDGE WEATHER STATION (same as key W).

④ THE 7-SEGMENT MIRROR

The little LED mirrors the physical hardware display: 0-9 health tens digit · C combat · H/n hub day/night · F laptop · b calibrating · L logging · R radio · M map · U binoculars · ! alert · S sanity critical · P/E pass/fail ending. It flashes red in any danger state — low health, sieges, sanity collapse, hijacked radio.

⑤ WATCHERS OF DEAD RIDGE (story mode)

Pick it from the story menu (page 2 via combo 1+4). You are the watchman of Fire-Tower 2 for 30 days. Submit an enviro-log every day: run Barometric Calibration on the laptop first, steer the deviation to ±2 mb with the << / < / > / >> buttons, then LOCK SYNC (SPACE) and enter the validation string in the Log Engine.
The trap: when the SOUTH ARRAY reads 1013 mb / MIMICRY INTERFERENCE, that value is the creature exhaling on the sensor — exclude it and calibrate on North + Valley only.
The Living Window: Watch the Windows → Focus Binoculars gives real intel while Sanity ≥ 50; below that the glass starts lying — false silhouettes, lit towers, your friends standing outside when they're inside. Crew Check-in reads each companion's live state: they argue, break down, and comment on what just happened (no more repeated lines).
Tune the shortwave (channels 14/88/104) — every listen feeds the learning meter; once it crosses ~55 the entity hijacks channels in your companions' own voices. If it speaks, don't answer. Explore the overworld (Tab / combo 1+2) — the world changes: bridges snap, cabins burn, new clearings appear, and around Day 13 Ranger Cole arrives to test. Press W for the Weather Station. Reach Day 30 with logs, friends and your mind — and never answer — for the true endings.

⑥ SURVIVAL TIPS

· Food & water tick down every dawn — forage or starve.
· Fortify before night: barricades cut siege odds hard.
· Sanity (Resolve) is a real resource in Dead Ridge: keep it above 30 or the segment display turns into an alarm.
· If an email, radio voice, or friend asks you to open something — read it twice first.
